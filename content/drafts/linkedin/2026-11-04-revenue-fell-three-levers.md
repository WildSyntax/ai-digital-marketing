Revenue fell last week. Which of three things broke?

Revenue is sessions, times conversion rate, times average order value.

So when it moves, only one of three levers moved.

Fewer people arrived.

Fewer of them bought.

Or each buyer spent less.

A worked example from Kanal's guide: 20,000 sessions at a 2.5% conversion rate gives 500 orders. At $60 each, that is $30,000.

Now suppose revenue is down 12% and orders are down 11%. Average order value barely moved. The problem is the number of buyers, so you check traffic by channel next.

If paid traffic fell, look at your ad budget or approvals.

If traffic held and conversion dropped, look at the site itself.

Here is the habit that makes this work:

1. Copy your nine weekly numbers into one sheet.
2. Add the change versus last week.
3. Flag anything that moved more than 10%.
4. Write one sentence on the likely cause.
5. Pick one action and one owner.

Twenty minutes, same slot every week. Monday at 9am works.

You don't need forty charts. You need to know which lever moved.
