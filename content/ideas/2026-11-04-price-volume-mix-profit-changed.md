## Angle
Revenue can stay flat while profit falls because customers shifted to lower-margin products. This post explains price-volume-mix analysis in plain terms for marketing leads who read one weekly report.

## Why now
Price-volume-mix is now a standard way to split gross profit changes, but explanations are written for finance teams, not small stores (https://www.vendavo.com/all/how-to-conduct-margin-impact-analysis/).

## Outline
- A real-looking example: flat sales, lower profit
- Splitting the change into price, volume and mix
- Doing it with two exports and a pivot table
- What to do when mix is the cause
