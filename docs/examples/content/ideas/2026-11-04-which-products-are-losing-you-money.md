## Angle
A best-selling product can lose money once returns, discounts and freight are counted. This post gives store teams a four-cost check they can run on their top 20 products in under an hour.

## Why now
One margin guide says freight, returns and discounts should be captured at the order-line level for accurate product margins, which is where hidden losses sit (https://www.cleverence.com/articles/for-business/margin-analysis-definition-5629/).

- Pull the numbers from your store export
