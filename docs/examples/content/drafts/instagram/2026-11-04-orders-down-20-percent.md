## Caption
Orders down 20% this week? Don't wait.

Shero Commerce suggests a week-over-week drop of 20% or more calls for immediate investigation.

A 5 to 10% dip can be normal. Compare with last week, not last year.

Weekly is the sweet spot: daily numbers are noisy, monthly reviews are too slow.
